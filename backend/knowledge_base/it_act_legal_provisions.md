# Statutory Legal Provisions: Information Technology Act, 2000 & Indian Penal Sanctions

## Document Reference: LEGAL/IT-ACT-2000/COMP-SOP
**Jurisdiction:** Republic of India  
**Applicable Statutes:** Information Technology Act, 2000 (Amended 2008), Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) / Indian Penal Code (IPC)

---

### Section 1: Offenses Pertaining to Computer Systems & Digital Identity
1. **Section 66C of IT Act (Identity Theft):**
   - *Statutory Definition:* Whoever, fraudulently or dishonestly, makes use of the electronic signature, password, or any other unique identification feature of any other person, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to three years and shall also be liable to fine which may extend to rupees one lakh.
   - *Invocation Context:* Unauthorized access using stolen login credentials, ATM PIN harvesting, cloned social media profiles, and forged digital signatures.
2. **Section 66D of IT Act (Cheating by Personation using Computer Resource):**
   - *Statutory Definition:* Whoever, by means of any communication device or computer resource cheats by personating, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to three years and fine up to rupees one lakh.
   - *Invocation Context:* Fake bank managers calling victims, caller ID spoofing, fraudulent lottery emails, and fake courier delivery executive impersonations.
3. **Section 43A of IT Act (Compensation for Failure to Protect Sensitive Personal Data):**
   - Imposes strict civil liability on corporate bodies possessing sensitive personal data (SPI) who fail to implement reasonable security practices, leading to wrongful loss or wrongful gain.

---

### Section 2: Online Harassment, Stalking, and Non-Consensual Imagery
1. **Section 66E of IT Act (Violation of Privacy):**
   - Capturing, publishing, or transmitting images of private area of any person without consent. Imprisonment up to 3 years or fine up to ₹2 lakh.
2. **Section 67 & 67A of IT Act (Publishing Obscene / Sexually Explicit Material in Electronic Form):**
   - Prescribes up to 5 years imprisonment and ₹10 lakh fine for publishing or transmitting sexually explicit conduct electronically.
3. **Section 354D IPC / Section 78 BNS (Cyber Stalking):**
   - Monitoring a woman’s use of the internet, email, or electronic communication with intent to harass or intimidate.

---

### Section 3: Extortion and Cyber Terrorism
1. **Section 66F of IT Act (Cyber Terrorism):**
   - Invocable in ransomware attacks impacting critical national information infrastructure (power grids, defense systems, major healthcare networks). Punishable with imprisonment for life.
2. **Section 384/385 IPC / BNS provisions on Extortion:**
   - Applicable in ransomware demands, black-mail threats, and sextortion schemes demanding monetary payoff.
