# Threat Indicators & Modus Operandi Typology Reference Manual

## Document Reference: THREAT-INTEL/TYPOLOGY/2026-IND
**Source Authority:** Cyber Threat Intelligence & Analytics Division  
**Scope:** Emerging Cyber Crime Modus Operandi in India and Behavioral Red Flags

---

### Section 1: "Digital Arrest" & Fake Law Enforcement Coercion Scam
1. **Modus Operandi:**
   - Fraudsters initiate video calls via WhatsApp or Skype wearing counterfeit police or customs uniforms with simulated police station backdrops.
   - Victims are informed that a parcel containing narcotics, passports, or illegal contraband has been seized at an international airport in their name.
   - Attackers assert that an arrest warrant has been issued by CBI, ED, or Mumbai Police, placing the victim under "digital arrest" and forbidding them from disconnecting the call.
   - Victims are coerced into transferring their lifetime savings into "government verification escrow accounts" (which are actually mule accounts).
2. **Key Threat Indicators:**
   - Urgent demand to stay continuously on video call for hours without alerting family members.
   - Forged letters bearing simulated official seals of Supreme Court, ED, or CBI.
   - Demand to transfer funds to a private bank account for "financial audit clearance".

### Section 2: Part-Time Job, Review Rating, and Telegram Investment Schemes
1. **Modus Operandi:**
   - Victims receive unsolicited WhatsApp messages offering flexible daily earnings (₹2,000 to ₹10,000) for simple tasks such as rating Google Maps locations, liking YouTube videos, or reviewing hotels.
   - Initial small payouts (₹150 to ₹500) are credited to establish credibility.
   - Victims are moved to private Telegram groups showcasing faked member withdrawals.
   - High-yield "prepaid tasks" or "crypto arbitrage trading" are introduced requiring deposits of ₹50,000 to ₹10,00,000. Once deposited, withdrawals are blocked and "tax clearance deposits" are demanded.
2. **Key Threat Indicators:**
   - Initial unsolicited WhatsApp invite leading to Telegram channels.
   - Promised returns exceeding 20% within 24 to 48 hours.
   - Requirement to deposit personal funds to unlock earned commissions.

### Section 3: Screen-Sharing & Remote Access APK Frauds
1. **Modus Operandi:**
   - Attackers pose as customer support representatives resolving utility bill delays, SIM KYC blocks, or banking app glitches.
   - The victim is guided to download an Android APK file via WhatsApp or install remote desktop software (AnyDesk, TeamViewer QuickSupport).
   - Once remote access is established, the victim is asked to make a nominal ₹5 test transaction, allowing the attacker to observe screen PINs and intercept incoming OTPs silently.
2. **Key Threat Indicators:**
   - Instruction to install remote desktop utilities on a smartphone to resolve a billing issue.
   - Delivery of APK files directly through chat apps rather than Google Play Store.
   - Direct monitoring of banking app credential inputs.
